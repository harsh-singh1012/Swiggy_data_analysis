# RFM Segmentation Summary

Generated by `python/03_rfm_segmentation.py`.

Analysis date used for recency: 2020-06-27 (day after the last order in the data).

Scoring is quintile-based (`pd.qcut` on rank, 1=worst, 5=best) computed from the actual distribution of each metric — not fixed business thresholds. Segments are assigned from the R and F scores (Monetary describes segment value rather than driving assignment, consistent with 'total revenue alone doesn't explain customer quality').

## Segment definitions used

- **Champions**: R score >= 4 and F score >= 4 — ordered recently and often.
- **Loyal / Active**: F score >= 4 (regardless of recency) — high lifetime order count.
- **Recent / New**: R score >= 4 and F score <= 2 — recently acquired, not yet frequent.
- **At Risk**: R score <= 2 and F score >= 3 — used to order regularly, gone quiet.
- **Low Engagement**: everyone else — low recency and low frequency.
