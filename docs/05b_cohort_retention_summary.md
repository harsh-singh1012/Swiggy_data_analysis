# Cohort / Retention Analysis Summary

Generated by `python/04_cohort_retention.py`.

Cohorts are defined by a customer's first-order month; `period_number` counts months since that first order. `M0` is always 100% by construction (every customer is in their own cohort in their acquisition month) — the numbers that matter are M1 onward.

- Month-1 retention (all cohorts, weighted): **4.6%** of customers who ordered in their acquisition month placed another order the very next month.
- Retention decays further from there — see `data/cleaned/cohort_retention_overall_curve.csv` for the full curve and `eda_charts/10_cohort_retention_heatmap.png` for the per-cohort view.
- This is consistent with the RFM finding that 42.5% of customers fall into Low Engagement and the order-frequency distribution's fast decay — most customers do not return, and the ones who do (Champions/Loyal) are the ones carrying disproportionate revenue.
- The dataset's reliable customer-date field (`order_date`) fully supports this analysis; no cohort had to be excluded for missing dates.
